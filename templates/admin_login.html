For this demo, password resets are managed in Render:

  1. Open your service in the Render dashboard and go to Environment.
  2. Set ADMIN_PASSWORD to a new, strong password. Do not change FLASK_SECRET_KEY.
  3. Choose Save, rebuild, and deploy, then sign in with the new password after deployment completes.

Keep the new password private. This app does not send password-reset emails.
